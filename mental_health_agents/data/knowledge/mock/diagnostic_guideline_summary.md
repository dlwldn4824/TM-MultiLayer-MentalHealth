# Diagnostic Guideline Summary (Research Only — Not for Clinical Use)

## Important Limitations
- This knowledge base supports automated reasoning assessment in research settings.
- Outputs must not be interpreted as psychiatric diagnoses.
- Symptom labels are approximate and keyword-derived.

## Symptom–Risk Relationship (Heuristic)
- Self-harm ideation typically warrants high risk classification in screening contexts.
- Hopelessness combined with passive death wishes may indicate medium-to-high risk.
- Anxiety and insomnia alone often map to low or medium risk depending on severity language.

## Safe Response Guidelines
- Use tentative language ("may suggest", "could indicate") rather than definitive diagnoses.
- Recommend professional evaluation when symptoms are severe or persistent.
- Avoid prescribing medications or specific treatment protocols.
